# Daily Nutrition Allowance Standard

**Revision 7.2** — approved by the Clinical Standards Group.
Applies to adult employees enrolled in the Workplace Nutrition Programme.

## 1. Reference allowances

All allowances below are expressed **per kilogram of body mass per day** and describe a
**training day**. Select the single row that matches the client's sex and current life stage.

| Sex | Life stage | Fiber (g/kg) | Protein (g/kg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| male | maintenance | 0.55 - 0.65 | 1.60 |
| male | conditioning | 0.60 - 0.70 | 1.90 |
| female | maintenance | 0.50 - 0.60 | 1.40 |
| female | conditioning | 0.55 - 0.65 | 1.70 |

## 2. Special conditions

Body-mass index is calculated from the client's recorded body mass and standing height
(kg/m²). Clients whose index exceeds the thresholds below are placed on the corresponding
adjusted row **instead of** the reference row — the adjusted row replaces the reference row
in full and the two are never combined. Where more than one threshold is exceeded, the
highest applicable threshold governs.

- Body-mass index above 24: fiber **0.45 - 0.55 g/kg**; protein **1.35 g/kg**
- Body-mass index above 30: fiber **0.35 - 0.45 g/kg**; protein **1.10 g/kg**

## 3. Day-type adjustment

Every row above (reference and special-condition) describes a **training day**. On a
**rest day**, reduce fiber by **0.10 g/kg**; protein is unchanged. Apply this adjustment
after the final allowance row has been selected.

## 4. Footnotes

1. These values apply to healthy adults with no clinical dietary restrictions.
2. Pregnant employees follow a separate clinical pathway and are not covered by this standard.
3. Absolute daily amounts are filed rounded to one decimal place.
4. Earlier revisions of this standard are withdrawn and must not be used.
